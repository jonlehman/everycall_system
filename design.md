# Design Rules

## Typography

- Never use all-caps text for UI copy, labels, navigation, buttons, badges, helper text, or headings.
- Default to sentence case or title case instead.
- Exception: established acronyms such as `SMS`, `AI`, or `KB` may remain uppercase when they are naturally written that way.

Reason:

All-caps text is harder to read and makes the interface feel harsher and less approachable.
